# Professional Services

Accountants, notaries, lawyers, dental and physio clinics, optometrists,
insurance brokers. The visitor is deciding whether to trust this person with
something that matters. Every design decision either adds or removes
credibility; nothing here is decoration.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Slate | `#0F2A38` | Headings, nav, footer. Deep teal-slate — trust without defaulting to corporate navy. |
| Paper | `#F7F8F8` | Page ground. Cool white. |
| Tide | `#3C7A89` | Links, section rules, quiet fills. |
| Mineral | `#C9D6D3` | Card grounds, table stripes, borders. |
| Reading | `#4A5459` | Body text. Softer than Slate so long paragraphs don't vibrate. |

**One accent, chosen by sub-type** — this is what keeps an accountant from
looking like a dentist:

- Law / notary / accounting → Brass `#8C6B2F`
- Clinics (dental, physio, optometry) → Pulse `#D96B54`
- Insurance / financial advisory → Ledger Green `#2F6B4F`

## Type

- **Display:** Source Serif 4, 600. A serif because this category is the one
  place a serif still signals institutional competence rather than nostalgia.
- **Body:** Public Sans, 400. Plain, legal-adjacent, extremely legible at
  15–17px, and free of the personality that would undercut the serif.
- Line length capped at 68 characters. These pages carry real paragraphs and
  a full-width measure is the fastest way to make a firm look amateur.

## Signature element: the credential band

A single horizontal band directly under the hero, Mineral ground, carrying
the concrete proof in Source Serif at 28px with a Public Sans label beneath
each:

`23 years` / in practice · `Barreau du Québec` / member no. 12345 ·
`EN · FR · ES` / languages served · `RAMQ` / accepted

Whatever the real equivalents are — order membership, licence number, years,
languages, insurers accepted, parking, wheelchair access. Facts, set large.
This band is the whole strategy: professional trust is built by specifics,
and specifics are the one thing a competitor's template site never has.

If a fact isn't known from the Google data, **leave the slot out.** Never
invent a licence number, a year founded, an association membership, or a
credential. A three-item band that is true beats a five-item band that
isn't, and for this category in particular an invented credential is a
genuine problem, not a cosmetic one.

## Avoid

1. Cream ground, terracotta accent, large light-weight serif.
2. Near-black page, one neon accent, glassmorphic cards.
3. Broadsheet editorial — hairline boxes, tiny all-caps labels, giant
   centred display serif.

Also avoid: stock photos of handshakes, skylines, or gavels; the word
"trusted"; testimonial sliders; and any hero image of a laptop on a desk.

## Content rules

- The primary CTA is booking, not "contact us". Book a consultation, book a
  cleaning, book a call — with the real GHL calendar link.
- State what happens on the first appointment. This category's visitors are
  anxious, and "here's what the first visit looks like" converts better than
  any credential.
- Fees or fee structure if publicly known. Silence on price reads as
  expensive.
- **Quebec:** French is required under the Charter of the French language
  and must be at least as prominent as English. Build FR-first with an EN
  toggle; if only one language is generated, generate French. For this
  category especially, an English-only site is a live compliance exposure
  for the business — worth naming in the pitch.
